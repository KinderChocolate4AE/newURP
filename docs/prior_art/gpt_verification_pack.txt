PART A — CONTEXT & DIRECTION PIVOT
================================================================
Problem: last-mile counter-UAS. A heterogeneous defender team — several CHEAP KINETIC "path-limiter"
drones + ONE NON-DESTRUCTIVE "net-capturer" drone (finisher) — defends a point against a hostile drone.
Style: cooperative "shepherding" (path-limiters shape the adversary; the net-capturer finishes).

OLD framing (abandoned): "manufacturing capturability via cooperative shaping" — path-limiters herd the
adversary into the net-capturer's dynamic capture-viability set. WHY ABANDONED: concept-precedented
(see Part B threats Von Moll turret+defender; Chen luring). "First flag on a place others reach in ~2yr"
is not a moat.

NEW load-bearing claim: **cooperative SHAPING used as a LEVER on the EXCHANGE-ECONOMICS of a
FINITE, IRREVERSIBLE, MISS-IS-FREE non-destructive (net) finisher — solved by MARL.**

Root structural hook (MISS-IS-FREE ASYMMETRY): a missed net is COSTLESS to the attacker but spends one
of the defender's SCARCE, irreversible shots (deploy delay τ, finite magazine). Kinetic defense lacks
this (a near-miss still deters/damages). So a smart attacker BAITS/EXHAUSTS the nets, then penetrates —
geometry/"capturability" alone is meaningless if you can dodge the net. The research question is NOT
"can we catch this drone" (a tactic, loses the spear-vs-shield loop) but "what is the COST/EXCHANGE
structure, and in which resource regimes (net capacity × speed/agility ratio × team size × net spec)
does a shaping defense have positive value AT ALL, robust to the attacker's best response (incl.
exhaustion/baiting)" — a statement about the game's payoff structure, not a tactic.

Ethos: usability > fidelity (3DOF KINEMATIC sim; we do NOT compete on 6-DoF realism; we make PARAMETERS
realistic: net capacity, deploy time/spread/range, commercial/FPV speeds). Novelty defended only as an
INTERSECTION. Moat = publish the frame, hold the recipe (tuned policy, full quantitative envelope,
stack). arXiv priority is the keystone (space is closing fast).

Carry-forward asset: a surrogate E_req = w1·||v_perp||^2 + w2·||lambda_dot||^2 + w3·max(0, a_req -
a_net,max)^2 + w4·||delta_p_net(tau_deploy)||^2  (v_perp = closing-normal vel; lambda_dot = LOS rate;
a_req margin vs net max accel; miss-during-deploy). Reframed from "capture difficulty" to PER-SHOT VALUE.
