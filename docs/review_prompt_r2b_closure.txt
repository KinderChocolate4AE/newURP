5. 정직 caveat (감사 반영 확정 문안 — 등록 정본)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  c1. p_C_hat = 봉인 search 절차의 attainment rate — 그 자체로는 정확한
      estimand. 물리적 achievability 확률 아님: search misses (nosol 포함) 는
      infeasibility 로 해석 불가; observed replay successes 는 existence
      witness 이므로 관측 성공률은 search-class feasibility 의 witness-based
      lower bound 일 뿐이다 [감사 c1 정정 반영].
  c2. C plan 은 scenario 별 privileged open-loop search 산물 (공격자 closed-
      loop 반응 포함). 배치가능 controller 가 아니다. "upper bound" 어휘 금지
      (retired) — "search-based attainment/achievability benchmark" 만.
  c3. per-scenario C_N >= B_N 비보장 (유한예산 stochastic + lite proxy 선별).
      Δp_CB 는 descriptive 전용.
  c4. [감사 문안] The FULL branch was selected in procedural compliance with
      the sealed branch rule, but the runtime estimator was mis-specified
      (elapsed/len(records) 가 CEM 없는 싼 record 를 희석) and underestimated
      realized cost by ~4x (8.71 h -> ~37 h); **therefore the intended 14-h
      resource gate was not actually satisfied.** This affected resource
      accounting, not the already completed outcome comparison. 차기 threshold
      는 per-CEM-solve 실측으로 교정.
  c5. 판정 = scenario 당 full-fidelity replay 1회 (결정론이므로 반복 불요).
  c6. 세계 동결 유지: 결론은 M_3DOF sealed world 내부. 6DOF 는 docs/93 gate.
  c7. [감사 BLOCKER 1] C arm does not estimate a shifted chi50 or a new
      capture boundary. It measures search-pipeline NET_CAPTURE attainment on
      the pre-specified boundary-local S_C scenarios. +0.370 을 "경계를 0.37
      밀었다" 로 읽는 것 금지.
  c8. [감사 BLOCKER 2] The C-vs-A gain is not decomposed into single-limiter,
      independent multi-limiter, or genuinely cooperative components;
      C_POSITIVE establishes exploitable limiter-control opportunity under
      the search procedure, **not necessity of cooperation.** (plan-class
      분해 §3 은 class 단위일 뿐 상호의존성 증거가 아니다.)
  c9. [감사 BLOCKER 3] The C-positive numerical rule was not part of B0; it
      was locked before recorded C-outcome retrieval by mechanical
      transplantation of the P1 rule. It must not be described as
      B0-preregistered. (동일 지위: proxy = pre-execution locked.)

6. claim 등록 정본 (감사 확정 문안 — 이대로 등록)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C047 (P1) [감사 PASS-w-edits 반영]: Under the frozen R2b world, rule-based
    limiter interception did not meet the pre-specified P1 positive-effect
    criterion for NET_CAPTURE (paired global Δp_net = −0.001, 95% CI
    [−0.004, +0.001]; 5/14 rows positive). In the low-η cells, decreases in
    NET_CAPTURE were accompanied by approximately offsetting increases in
    HARD_KILL, while total-neutralization change was near zero. No Δchi50
    boundary shift was estimated.
  C048 (C) [감사 PASS-w-edits 반영]: On the pre-specified paired S_C scenario
    IDs, the sealed-budget CEM limiter-plan search pipeline (384 lite
    rollouts/scenario; one deterministic full-fidelity replay for final
    judgment; NET_CAPTURE-only primary success) produced a NET_CAPTURE
    attainment increase over hold of Δp_CA_net = +0.370, 95% CI
    [+0.353, +0.388], with positive contrasts in 14/14 rows and both λ
    slices. Per-cell HARD_KILL under C was ≤ 0.14. This is a search-based
    attainment benchmark, not a deployable policy or an estimated Δchi50.
  C049 (해석) [감사 FAIL -> 교체 문안]: The R2b P1 null result does not
    support the stronger interpretation that limiter control offers no
    exploitable opportunity in the sealed M_3DOF world: the rule-based
    intercept arm failed the pre-specified positive-effect criterion, while
    the privileged sealed-budget limiter-plan search achieved substantially
    higher paired NET_CAPTURE attainment. The result motivates evaluation of
    more expressive deployable controllers, including learned cooperative
    controllers, but does not establish that cooperation itself is necessary,
    that the observed gain is a cooperative-geometry mechanism, or that MARL
    will recover the search benchmark.
